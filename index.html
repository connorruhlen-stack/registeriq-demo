Around the app

Each picture opens full size. The demo store has every page shown here, and you can click through it.

The dashboard says what stood out at the register this week, in plain words.

Each finding shows the transactions behind it. The next step is in the margin.

The owner marks it resolved or dismisses it, with a note for the books.

Stock entries are checked too, like a delivery typed in wrong.

Every two weeks or once a month, the entries to check come together in one report. It downloads as a PDF for whoever does the books.

Where it’s at

RegisterIQ is in its pilot. For now it runs on realistic sample data and on sales and stock files a shop exports from its till. Connecting to a till directly comes next, starting with Square.

It’s made for independent shops with one to five locations.
